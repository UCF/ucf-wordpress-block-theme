# Design principles

The content and design rules this theme's patterns are written to, from the UCF Web Design
System. The code enforces what it can — square corners, the focus ring, contrast through the
compositions. Everything here is what it cannot, so it falls to whoever writes a pattern or a
page.

## Principles

1. **One job per page.** Know why someone is on the page and remove what sits between them
   and it. One primary action, visible without scrolling, on a phone.
2. **Answer first.** The first sentence of a page or a section is the answer. Context follows.
3. **Every fact carries its source.** A number either belongs to this page, with a source and
   a date, or it links to the office that owns it. Don't restate another page's facts.
4. **Mobile is the page.** Design for 390px first. Nothing is hidden on a small screen.
5. **Accessible is finished.** WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor.

## Writing

-   Headings in **sentence case**. The theme sets display and H1–H3 in capitals; never type
    them. Keep those under about ten words.
-   Phrase H2s as the question the section answers: "What do graduates do?", not "Outcomes".
-   Link and button text says what happens next: "Start your application", never "Learn
    more", "Click here" or a bare URL.
-   Write a statistic as a complete statement — "Florida's largest university, with 70,674
    students" — with one supporting sentence and a source. Never a big number over small words.
-   Write dates out (Sept. 15, 2026).
-   Leave internal structure out of public copy: a prospective student doesn't know what a
    college, school or unit means here.

## Color and surfaces

-   Pick a composition for a band — Light, Light Gray, Dark, Bold Gold — and nothing else.
    Editors never choose a text or button color.
-   Balance light and dark down the page. At most one gold band, usually the closing call to
    action.
-   Gold is a fill, never text on a light ground. Links are always underlined.
-   Danger and success always appear with an icon and a word.

## Type voices

-   **Oswald**: display and H1–H3, in capitals.
-   **Montserrat**: everything else, in sentence case. Use the Sans heading style when a
    heading needs the H4 look at a higher level.
-   **Roboto Mono** (the Data paragraph style): only for things a reader can check — sources,
    dates, figure notes. Never a running paragraph.

## Imagery

-   Real UCF people, places and work. A photo that could belong to any university belongs to
    none.
-   Ratios: 3:2 default, 16:9 video, 1:1 portraits, 4:5 vertical, 21:9 wide banners — the
    presets in the image toolbar.
-   Budgets: hero 300 KB at 1920px, inline 150 KB at 1200px, card 60 KB at 600px, page under
    2 MB. Width and height always set.
-   Alt text says what the image shows and why it is there; decorative images take empty
    alt. No text baked into images.
-   No carousels, sliders or rotating heroes. No tabs.

## Icons

Icons clarify an action or a kind of thing — download, external, date, location. They never
decorate, never sit on every link, and are never the only label for a control.
